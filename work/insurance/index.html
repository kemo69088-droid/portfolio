Read-out

What the data says

  1. 01

    Smoking is the price driver

    Smokers pay 3.8× more on average ($32,050 vs $8,434). Correlation with charges is 0.79.

  2. 02

    Obese smokers are the costliest segment

    Smokers with BMI 30+ average $41,558, 4.7× an obese non-smoker. A quit-smoking programme pays back fastest here.

  3. 03

    Age is a steady second factor

    Within each band charges climb with age, so age should price the base rate and smoking the multiplier.

  4. 04

    Region and sex can be dropped from pricing talks

    Their effect is a few thousand dollars at most, inside the noise of the other factors.

Method & definitions

  • Charges = individual annual medical costs billed by the insurer (USD).
  • BMI classes follow WHO cut-offs: under 25, 25 to 29.9, 30 and over.
  • Correlation uses smoker = 1, male = 1. All 1,338 rows kept, the single duplicate row does not change any figure shown.
