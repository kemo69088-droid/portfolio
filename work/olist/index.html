Read-out

What the data says

  1. 01

    Fix delivery before buying more traffic

    Orders that arrive late lose 1 to 2.6 review stars. Cutting the 6.8% late share is the cheapest way to lift the 4.10 average rating.

  2. 02

    Customers buy once and leave

    Only 3.0% of 94,703 customers placed a second order. A retention offer after the first delivery is the largest untapped lever.

  3. 03

    Plan stock and staff around November

    Black Friday produced 7.4× a normal day's revenue in a single day. Carriers and sellers need capacity booked ahead.

  4. 04

    Focus marketing where demand already is

    SP, RJ and MG generate 63% of revenue, and health beauty leads all categories at R$ 1.25M.

Method & definitions

  • Revenue = item price + freight for orders not cancelled or unavailable, purchased Jan 2017 – Aug 2018 (97,905 orders).
  • Review score = latest review per order. On time = delivered on or before the estimated date (delivered orders only).
  • Repeat customers = unique customer IDs with two or more orders in the period.

Data: Olist public dataset (Kaggle)
